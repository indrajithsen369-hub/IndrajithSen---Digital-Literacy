Stay Safe Online Check list

General Online Safety

• Use strong passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
• Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on email, social media, and banking apps.
• Do not click on unknown links sent through SMS, email, or WhatsApp.
• Do not share personal information like address, college ID, phone number, or passwords online.
• Always download apps and software from official websites or app stores only.
• Check privacy settings on your social media accounts and keep them private if possible.

Financial / UPI Safety

• Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone — even if they claim to be from the bank.
• Remember: UPI PIN is only used to send money, not to receive money.
• Always verify the name of the receiver before sending money.
• Do not scan unknown QR codes as they may deduct money from your account.

If You Become a Victim of Cybercrime

• Immediately report the fraud on the National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
• Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 as soon as possible to try to stop the transaction.
• Inform your bank immediately if any fraudulent transaction happens.
