# IndrajithSen---Digital-Literacy
Name   : Indrajith Sen
Reg no : 25BET10015
A comprehensive project showcasing digital citizenship, professional online presence, and cybersecurity awareness. Includes modules on personal branding, digital tools, and ethical communication.
This repository contains my Digital Literacy Project created as part of the CSE0001 Digital Literacy course. The purpose of this project is to develop essential digital skills that are important for academic growth, professional development, and responsible online behavior.
This project includes five main tasks. In Task 1, I created a Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic to explain the importance of digital literacy and safe internet practices. In Task 2, I built my digital portfolio by creating professional profiles on platforms like GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle. In Task 3, I explored coding and collaboration platforms by completing a beginner coding challenge on HackerRank and creating a Google Form for a Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz. In Task 4, I wrote professional emails and created a social media Do’s and Don’ts checklist to understand proper email etiquette and responsible online communication. In Task 5, I completed a cybercrime case study on UPI and Online Payment Fraud and created a Stay Safe Online checklist.
Through this project, I learned about digital tools, online safety, professional communication, and the importance of building a professional online presence. This project helped me improve my technical, communication, and digital literacy skills.
Module 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness
In this module, I learned the meaning and importance of digital literacy. Digital literacy is the ability to use digital devices, the internet, and online platforms safely and effectively. I created an infographic to explain topics such as safe internet practices, useful digital tools for students, and professional online presence. This module helped me understand how important digital skills are in today’s world for education and career growth.
Module 2 – Building a Digital Portfolio
In this module, I created my professional digital portfolio using platforms like GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle. I learned how these platforms are used to showcase projects, connect with professionals, and practice technical skills. This module helped me understand the importance of maintaining a professional online presence and building a strong portfolio for future internships and job opportunities.
Module 3 – Coding and Collaboration Platforms
In this module, I explored coding practice platforms and collaboration tools. I completed a beginner-level coding challenge on HackerRank to improve my programming and problem-solving skills. I also created a Google Form and used Google Sheets to collect responses. This module helped me understand how online platforms can be used for learning, collaboration, and data collection.
Module 4 – Email Etiquette and Online Communication
In this module, I learned how to write professional emails and communicate properly in an academic or professional environment. I wrote emails to a professor and an internship coordinator and created a social media Do’s and Don’ts checklist. This module helped me understand the importance of polite, clear, and professional digital communication.
Module 5 – Cybercrime Awareness and Online Safety
In this module, I studied cybercrime, especially UPI and online payment fraud. I wrote a case study and created a Stay Safe Online checklist. This module helped me understand common online scams, how they happen, and how to protect myself and others from cybercrime.
