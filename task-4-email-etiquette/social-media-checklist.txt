5 Do's or Don'ts for responsible social media as a college student

Do's

• Do think before you post anything online.
• Do keep your account privacy settings secure.
• Do use social media for learning and networking.
• Do share positive and useful content.
• Do respect others’ opinions and be polite online.

Don'ts

• Don’t share personal information like address, phone number, or passwords.
• Don’t post offensive, rude, or harmful comments.
• Don’t spend too much time on social media and ignore studies.
• Don’t believe or share fake news without checking.
• Don’t post anything that can harm your reputation or future career.
